TICKET-005: Increased Latency on Product Search Endpoint

Priority: Low
Status: Open
Reported: 2024-03-19

Summary:
P95 response time on the product search endpoint has increased from 180ms to 620ms over the past week with no corresponding increase in traffic volume.

Observations:
- Slowdown appears consistent across all query types
- Database query execution time is unchanged
- Increase correlates with product catalog growth from 12,000 to 31,000 items
- Search is currently performing a full table scan with in-memory filtering

Next Steps:
Evaluate adding a search index on the product catalog. Consider Elasticsearch or PostgreSQL full-text search as alternatives to the current implementation.
