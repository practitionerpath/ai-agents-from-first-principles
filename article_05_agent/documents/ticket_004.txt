TICKET-004: Memory Leak in Notification Service

Priority: Medium
Status: In Progress
Reported: 2024-03-18

Summary:
The notification service is showing steady heap memory growth over 24 hour periods, eventually triggering OOM restarts approximately every 36 hours.

Observations:
- Heap usage grows from roughly 200MB at startup to 1.8GB before OOM kill
- Growth rate increases during periods of high email volume
- Heap dump analysis shows accumulation of unreleased email template objects
- Suspected cause: template cache is not evicting stale entries

Next Steps:
Review template caching implementation. Evaluate adding TTL-based eviction or bounding the cache size.
