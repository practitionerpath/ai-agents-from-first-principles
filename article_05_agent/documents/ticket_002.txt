TICKET-002: Database Connection Pool Exhaustion on Orders Service

Priority: Medium
Status: Resolved
Resolved: 2024-03-10

Summary:
The orders service experienced intermittent 503 errors during peak traffic hours due to connection pool exhaustion on the primary database.

Root Cause:
Connection pool maximum was set to 10 connections, insufficient for peak load of approximately 450 requests per minute. Each request was holding connections open for an average of 2.3 seconds.

Resolution:
Connection pool maximum increased from 10 to 50. Added connection timeout of 30 seconds to prevent indefinite blocking. Deployed in release 3.1.2.
